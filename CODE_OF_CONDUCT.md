# Code of Conduct

This is a small space attached to a small project. The rules are short because they can be.

## What is expected

**Be decent to people.** Assume the person you are replying to is competent, is acting in good faith,
and knows something you do not. Most of the time all three are true.

**Criticise the work, not the person.** "This design breaks on ARM" is useful. "Whoever wrote this
clearly has not thought about ARM" is the same information with a person attached to it, and it is
worth less.

**Say what you actually did.** If you are reporting a bug, report what happened rather than what you
concluded from it. If you are answering one, say when you are guessing.

**Let people be new.** Someone asking a question you find obvious is not the problem. They are the
reason a Q&A section is worth having.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks. Including as a joke, and including in a direct message
  that started here.
- Discriminatory or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity: who they are, where they are from,
  what they believe, what their body does.
- Sexual content or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Deliberately derailing threads, spamming, or posting to advertise something unrelated.
- Sustained disruption after being asked to stop.

This applies to issues, discussions, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere else this project's
name is what put you in the room together.

## If something goes wrong

Email **contact@talosassistants.com**. Say what happened and link to it if it is public.

Reports are read by the maintainers and kept private. You will get a reply. We will not tell the
person you reported who reported them unless you say we may.

## What happens then

Depending on what happened and whether it is a pattern: a private word, an edited or deleted post, a
temporary block, or a permanent one. The maintainers decide, and will say which one and why.

Reporting something in good faith will never be held against you, even if we conclude no rule was
broken. Reporting something in bad faith, to silence someone or to retaliate, is itself a breach.
